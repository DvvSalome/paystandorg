Paystand Impact Foundation · Grant Application 2027

Funding for Bitcoin education and economic empowerment

We support organizations and individuals who use Bitcoin to expand financial access in their communities. Applications are completed online in one form.

Start your application

The form is in English. You can write your answers in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

What we support

Every project is aligned with at least one of our three pillars.

Education

Training, curricula and workshops that teach people how Bitcoin works and how to use it safely.

Economic Empowerment

Projects that help people and small businesses earn, save, trade and receive payments.

Technology Adoption

Infrastructure, wallets, nodes and open-source tools that make Bitcoin usable in local communities.

How it works

One online form, reviewed by our team and a committee.

  1. Tell us about youType of applicant, time operating and whether you received a grant in 2026.
  2. Describe your organization and projectLegal details, project goals, expected results and budget.
  3. Upload your documentsProof of registration and, if you have it, your tax form.
  4. Receive your application IDYou get a confirmation email with your ID once you submit.

Documents you will need

PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each.

Nonprofit or organization

  • Official registration
  • Proof of nonprofit status (NGOs)
  • Tax form W-9 (U.S.) or W-8BEN-E (outside the U.S.)

Individual

  • Valid official photo ID
  • Tax form W-9 (U.S.) or W-8BEN (outside the U.S.)

The tax form is optional when you apply and required if you are selected.
